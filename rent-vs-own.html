💡 What is the Wealth Gap - Owner Advantage?
This is the most important number in this comparison. It measures the total financial difference between owning and renting over the same period - taking everything into account: the equity built through mortgage payments and home appreciation, the opportunity cost of the down payment (modelled as a HISA investment if renting), and all cash spent in each scenario.

A positive number means the owner ends up ahead - even after accounting for higher monthly costs of ownership. This is common over 10+ years because mortgage payments build equity while rent is entirely spent. Over a full amortisation period, the gap compounds significantly as appreciation works on the full home value while the mortgage shrinks.

Note: Projections use fixed assumptions for appreciation, rent growth, and investment returns. Actual results will vary. This is illustrative, not financial advice.
